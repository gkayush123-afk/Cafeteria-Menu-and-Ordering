CAFEGO ARCHITECTURE MAP
=============================================================================
1. MENU & CUSTOMIZATIONS:
   - Route: GET /menu, GET /api/menu
   - Templates: index.html, menu.html
   - Logic: app.js (openCustomModal, dynamic spice & add-on pricing engine)
   - DB: menu_items (is_special, customization_options)

2. CART & CLIENT STATE:
   - Templates: cart.html
   - Logic: app.js (getCart, saveCart, dynamic calculations, badge counter)
   - Storage: Browser LocalStorage (key: "cafe_cart")

3. PRE-ORDER & CHECKOUT:
   - Route: GET/POST /checkout
   - Templates: checkout.html
   - DB: users, orders, order_items, pickup_slots, payments
   - Features: Time slot capacity validation, Cashless UPI QR/Card/Wallet

4. REAL-TIME TRACKING & NOTIFICATIONS:
   - Route: GET /tracking/<id>, GET /api/orders/<id>
   - Templates: tracking.html
   - Logic: app.js (initLiveOrderTracking, Web Push Notification API, Audio Chime)

5. RECEIPTS & ORDER HISTORY:
   - Routes: GET /receipt/<id>, GET /history
   - Templates: receipt.html, history.html
   - Features: Printable GST invoice, QR pickup verification, email search

6. ADMIN HUB & SALES ANALYTICS:
   - Routes: GET/POST /admin/login, GET /admin, /admin/status/<id>, /admin/toggle/<id>, /admin/special/<id>, /admin/item/add, /admin/slot/add
   - Templates: admin.html, admin_login.html
   - Analytics: Total Revenue, AOV, Popular Items ranked, Peak Slot loads
=============================================================================
